Reader Guide

Turning pages

Swipe left to go to the next page. Swipe right to go to the previous page. A stationary tap never turns the page.

Tap the middle to show or hide the reader controls. Swiping is the page-navigation gesture.

Bubble Zoom

  • Hold Screen → zoom the bubble in/out.
  • Double-tap a bubble → enlarge only the bubble.

Zooming & panning

  • Pinch → zoom the full page
  • Drag → pan while zoomed
  • Tap a panel → focus that frame
  • Double-tap while focused → return to full page

Layout

  • Page → one page at a time.
  • Spread → two pages side by side; requests landscape orientation when supported.
  • Scroll → continuous top-to-bottom reading.
  • Manga → continuous right-to-left reading.
  • Webcomic → continuous top-to-bottom reading for webcomics/manhua.

Theme & bookmark

The three circles switch between dark, sepia, and light reading themes. The registration-mark icon bookmarks your current page.
